# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static output, content collections for journal). Chosen on assistant recommendation: content-driven gallery + journal site, markdown-based blog needs no database/CMS, static output is fast and SEO-friendly, deploys to Dokploy via Dockerfile + nginx. User confirmed "English only" and "generate imagery first".

## Users

- International bonsai collectors and enthusiasts discovering Indonesian bonsai (esp. Santigi / Pemphis acidula) via search.
- Prospective buyers inquiring about private collection pieces, export, or wholesale/dealer collaboration.
- Visitors planning a trip to the Bali gallery (garden experience, private viewing, workshops).
- Exhibition collaborators, hotels, and event partners.

## Product Purpose

Luxury gallery website for Bonsai D'Uma Bali — not a marketplace. Establishes the gallery as the international authority on Indonesian bonsai, showcases the collection, documents exhibitions, publishes a journal for SEO authority, and routes serious inquiries (private collection, export, wholesale, collaboration, workshops) through contact forms.

## Positioning

A living gallery in Bali exporting museum-grade Indonesian bonsai worldwide. The journal + export documentation (phytosanitary, USA export process) is the moat: no other Indonesian gallery publishes this authority content in English.

## Operating Context

- Founded 2020 in Bali, Indonesia; began in the founder's front yard.
- Exports cultivated bonsai internationally (phytosanitary certified, USA export experience).
- Exhibitions: ROOTED @ ZIN Canggu, Pamnas, national/international exhibitions, hotel displays.
- Species focus: Sancang, Santigi, Ficus, Anting Putri, Sianci, Lohansung, Cemara, Ulmus.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- 11 sections: Home, Collection (filterable: species/size/style/status), International/Export, Garden, Artists, Exhibitions & Events, Journal, Services, Visit Us, Contact/Private Collection inquiry, Footer.
- English only (user decision, Oct 8 2026).
- Imagery: AI-generated placeholders first (user decision, Oct 8 2026); all synthetic imagery must be labeled/replaceable with real photography later. No invented commercial claims: no prices, no fake testimonials, no fake collector names.
- Collection pieces show: species (latin), origin, size, style, status (Available/Reserved/Sold/Exported).
- Inquiry form fields: Name, Country, Email/WhatsApp, Tree of Interest, Collection Purpose, Message. Purpose options: Private Collection, Exhibition, Export, Wholesale/Dealer, Collaboration, Workshop.
- No e-commerce checkout; inquiries route via form (front-end only for now — note as undecided backend).

## Product Principles

1. Gallery, not marketplace: the trees are art; the site seduces, never shouts.
2. Authority through documentation: journal and export process content earn international trust.
3. Every tree tells its story: species, origin, style, and status are always visible.
4. Bali is the soul: the garden, the hands, the atmosphere are always present.
5. Inquiry is the conversion: every path leads a serious visitor toward a conversation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
