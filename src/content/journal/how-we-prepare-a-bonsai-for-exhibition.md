An exhibition tree is not simply a beautiful tree. It is a tree at its peak — and peak is a window measured in days.

## Selection

Months before a show, we walk the garden asking one question: which trees are approaching their moment? Foliage density, ramification, the freshness of the last styling — a tree can be magnificent and still wrong for this particular exhibition.

## The final styling

Three to four weeks out, the tree receives its exhibition styling: every wire placed with intention, every pad defined, deadwood cleaned and treated. Nothing is rushed. A branch wired in haste will betray you under gallery light.

## The pot, the stand, the accent

The tree chooses its companions. Pot color against bark, stand height against trunk mass, an accent planting that echoes without competing. We try combinations the way a curator hangs a wall — then we live with them for days before deciding.

## The last week

Watering becomes ritual. Moss is refreshed, the pot is cleaned, the soil surface is dressed. On the morning of the show, the tree is inspected leaf by leaf.

Then we step back. The tree does the rest.
